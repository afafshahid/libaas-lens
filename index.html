    How it works

    From two public datasets and our own wardrobes to a model in the browser

    Most fashion models are trained mainly on Western clothing. We combine ethnic and Western garments in one label space and test the model on photos of clothes from our own homes in Karachi.

    1. Prepare the data

      Map public datasets to 10 shared labels, balance the classes and resize images to 224 × 224.

    2. Train a baseline

      MobileNetV2 pretrained on ImageNet, frozen, with a new classification layer.

    3. Fine-tune

      Unfreeze the top layers and train with a lower learning rate and simple augmentation.

    4. Evaluate

      Precision, recall, F1 and a confusion matrix, on the public test set and on our own photos.

    5. Deploy

      Convert the model to TensorFlow.js so it runs on this page.

    Five ethnic and five Western classes, chosen for what people in Pakistan actually wear.
