Methodology & Architecture

End-to-End Perception Pipeline

A principled transfer-learning workflow from multi-source dataset alignment through model fine-tuning to browser graph deployment.

Step 01

Data Alignment & Split

Mapped heterogeneous source taxonomies into 10 clean target classes. Cleaned via DCT perceptual hashing (threshold 4) and stratified 70/15/15 into 2,600 balanced images (260/class).

Step 02

Frozen Baseline

MobileNetV2 pretrained on ImageNet with frozen weights. Added embedded Rescaling, GlobalAveragePooling2D, Dropout(0.2), and Dense(10, softmax). Trained with Adam (1e-3).

Step 03

Transfer Fine-Tuning

Unfroze top 35 layers of MobileNetV2 with BatchNorm kept in inference mode. Applied random flip, rotation, zoom, and contrast with Adam (1e-5) and ReduceLROnPlateau.

Step 04

Empirical Evaluation

Evaluated on held-out 390-image test set across per-class precision, recall, macro F1, and full confusion matrix, highlighting ethnic vs Western perception nuances.

Step 05

TF.js Deployment

Clean SavedModel exported and converted to chunked TensorFlow.js Graph Model. Verified 20-image parity test (discrepancy ≤ 0.0001%) for zero-latency client execution.

Western Apparel

5 Classes · Fashion Product
Annotation & Perception Rules: Each image is categorized strictly by its primary visible garment. If an upper tunic garment lacks lower bottoms, it is classified as Kurta. Sleeveless waistcoats with mandarin band collars are classified as Nehru Jacket. Full-length structured ceremonial tunics with front button plackets and heavier brocade fabrics are categorized as Sherwani.
